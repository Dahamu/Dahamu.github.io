About

Design first, now building toward technology.

I spent years making things people look at. Now I want to understand the systems underneath them.

I'm Daham, from Sri Lanka. I've just finished my A/Levels at Kingswood College, and I've spent years in the school Media Network designing posters, editing audio and leading creative work for societies across the school.

Design is my strongest practical skill. Technology is where I'm headed: Computer Science, Cybersecurity and AI. I'm building that foundation now. What I bring is visual communication, a habit of finishing things, and a lot of curiosity.
