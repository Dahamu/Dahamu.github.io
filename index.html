About me

About

I just finished my A/Levels at Kingswood College, Kandy, and Photoshop is where most of my free time goes. It started when I joined the School Media Network — that's the actual turning point — and turned into designing posters and social graphics for my school's societies: astronomy, aviation, IT, media, and a couple of joint community projects along the way.

I haven't locked in a direction for after school yet — I'm keeping an open mind and looking into a few different paths. Until then, this site is mostly a place to keep the design work I'm proud of in one spot.

Currently

  • Main tool: Adobe Photoshop
  • Status: after A/L, exploring paths
